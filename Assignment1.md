| Problem Domain   | Problem                                          | Which data to collect                                                                               | How to store the data                                                                                      | Which insights/decisions we can make                                                                                             |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Education**    | Low academic performance among students          | Attendance records, test scores, assignment submissions, class participation, teacher evaluations   | Store in school database (SQL system) or structured Excel/CSV files; cloud storage for large-scale schools | Identify struggling students early, improve teaching strategies, monitor attendance impact on grades, personalize learning plans |
| **Vaccination**  | Efficient vaccine distribution during a pandemic | Patient records, vaccination status, age groups, location data, infection rates, hospital capacity  | Centralized government health database, secure electronic medical records systems                          | Identify high-risk areas, ensure fair vaccine distribution, track vaccination coverage, predict outbreak trends                  |
| **Productivity** | Low productivity in workplace or remote work     | Work hours, task completion rates, login/activity logs, deadlines, break times, performance reviews | Time-tracking tools, company productivity dashboards, spreadsheets for small teams                         | Improve workflow efficiency, identify peak productivity hours, reduce time wastage, optimize task assignments                    |
